# Billion landing page — design and content brief

Single source of truth for every worker on this feature. If a task spec and this file disagree, this file wins. Do not edit this file; if something here is impossible, say so in your report.

## 1. Who Billion is

Billion is an Egyptian software house that helps industrial factories adopt AI and modernize operations. Four words define the promise: smart, optimized, reliable, fast. Billion is the parent brand; Valor (valor-labs.com) is its sister company in agriculture, an Arabic-first site for Egyptian farmers and distributors whose headline is "نشأنا محليًا لنخدم عالميًا" (we grew locally to serve globally) and whose product includes an "Ask Our Engineer" AI assistant with weather tracking and market prices. Billion should read as the more enterprise, industrial-facing parent of that family: same local pride and engineer-not-salesman tone, more restraint.

## 2. Audience and tone

Egyptian factory owners, plant managers and operations directors. They read spec sheets, not startup blogs. They care about stoppages, scrap, energy per unit, changeover time, delivery dates, and whether a vendor will still answer the phone in month six. They have been burned by systems that never left the pilot.

Write plainly, in English, with specifics. Banned words: elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge, empower, delve, synergy, "AI-powered" as a headline. No invented statistics, client names, or client locations. Claims about Billion's own process are fine; claims of results are not.

## 3. Design read (for design-taste-frontend)

Reading this as: B2B industrial services landing for Egyptian factory owners and plant managers, with a calm engineering-report editorial language, leaning toward Tailwind + Newsreader/Geist + restrained scroll motion.

Archetype: "Editorial Luxury" texture (high-end-visual-design) crossed with the warm-monochrome, flat, typographic system of minimalist-ui. Think a Swiss engineering firm's annual report or a machine datasheet, not a SaaS site. Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 6, VISUAL_DENSITY 4, ART_DIRECTION 7.

Hard exclusions: no purple/blue AI glow, no mesh or default gradients, no glassmorphism, no centered-hero-over-dark-mesh, no three identical feature cards, no pill buttons on primary actions, no Lucide/FontAwesome/Material icons, no Inter/Roboto/Arial/Open Sans/Helvetica, no emoji, no stock photography, no fake dashboards with random numbers, no infinite-loop animations, no edge-to-edge sticky navbar glued to the top.

## 4. Visual system (pinned; mockups and build must match)

Palette (one palette for the whole page; dark sections reuse it inverted):
- Paper `#F5F2EC` canvas; Paper-2 `#EDE8DF` alternate surface; White `#FFFFFF` card surface
- Ink `#15130F` display text and dark section background; Ink-2 `#2B2822` body text; Graphite `#6B665C` secondary text
- Rule `rgba(21,19,15,0.10)` hairlines; on dark: `rgba(245,242,236,0.14)`
- Kiln `#B5502F` single accent (links, WhatsApp button, numbered indices, one underline); Kiln-hover `#9E4327`; Kiln-tint `#F3E4DD` for tags
- Optional film-grain overlay at 3% opacity on paper sections. Shadows: none, or diffuse at <= 4% opacity.

Typography (all via next/font/google, no runtime font requests):
- Display: Newsreader (variable, optical size on), weight 400 to 500, tracking -0.02em, line-height 1.05. H1 `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`, H2 `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`.
- Body and UI: Geist, 17px/1.6 body, 15px UI, weights 400/500.
- Metadata: Geist Mono, 12 to 13px, uppercase, tracking 0.08em, for eyebrows, indices ("01" to "07"), labels.

Layout: 8px base; container max 1280px; gutters 20px mobile, 40px desktop; section padding 96px mobile to 160px desktop; hairline rules as the main structural device; numbered sections; asymmetric grids allowed (bento for value props), everything collapses to one column under 768px with generous gaps. Corner radius 2 to 6px. Buttons: primary = solid Ink with Paper text; accent = solid Kiln with Paper text (WhatsApp only); secondary = hairline outline. Icons: inline SVG line primitives at 1.25px stroke, or Phosphor Light. Imagery: typography, color fields, hairline technical line drawings (a production line schematic, a data-flow diagram), and UI stills drawn in code. No photographs.

Nav: inset floating bar (not glued edge-to-edge), wordmark "Billion" set in Newsreader, links: What we do, Industries, How we work, Proof, and a Kiln WhatsApp button. Mobile: a full-height sheet menu.

## 7. Motion (the animate skill decides the values; this is intent only)

Hero text and panel: one staggered reveal on load. Sections: reveal once on scroll, translate no more than 12px, no re-trigger. Hover: near-imperceptible. Nothing loops. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all of it. Cheapest tool that works; a motion library only if CSS cannot do it.

## 8. Accessibility and mobile

Semantic landmarks, one H1, skip link, visible focus rings, AA contrast on every text/background pair (check Kiln on Paper for small text; use Ink for small text), alt text on every meaningful SVG, keyboard-operable mobile menu with focus trap and Escape. Mobile-native skill rules: `dvh` units, safe-area insets, no tap highlight flash, hover gated by `(hover: hover)`, 16px minimum input font, no horizontal scroll at 320px.
