# Billion landing page — design and content brief

Revised 2026-09-17 for the dark visual system; see docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-16-software-house-pivot-design.md sections 10 and 11.

## 1. Who Billion is

Billion is a software house based in Cairo whose standing point is performance optimized to the maximum, and whose promise is that it has a solution for anything it faces. Motto: **Think bigger. Think Billion.** Billion solves problems across multiple domains: AI products and agents, platforms and products, performance engineering, data and automation, industrial and operations software, and the hard problems nobody else wants. Industrial software is where Billion started and remains a specialty, but not the whole story. Valor Labs (valor-labs.com) is its sister company in agriculture, an Arabic-first site for Egyptian farmers and distributors whose headline is "نشأنا محليًا لنخدم عالميًا" (we grew locally to serve globally) and whose product includes an "Ask Our Engineer" AI assistant with weather tracking and market prices. Billion and Valor share the same local pride and engineer-not-salesman tone.

## 2. Audience and tone

Founders, product and engineering leads, and operations directors who need something built or made fast. They read spec sheets, not startup blogs. They care about shipping speed, reliability under load, cost at scale, and whether a vendor will still support the system in year three. They have been burned by consultants and platforms that shipped late or didn't scale.

Write plainly, in English, with specifics. Banned words: elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge, empower, delve, synergy, "AI-powered" as a headline. No invented statistics, client names, or client locations. Claims about Billion's own process are fine; claims of results are not.

## 3. Design read (for design-taste-frontend)

Reading this as: a dark, typographic B2B software house landing with deep space references and subtle interactivity, for founders and engineering leads who appreciate calm engineering-report language and intentional motion.

Archetype: minimalist tech, inspired by micro1.ai. One near-black background, white typography, a single warm accent, and a canvas effect triggered by user interaction (the word "Billion" brings a starfield to life). Think a dark terminal interface meeting a high-end showroom. Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 4, VISUAL_DENSITY 5, ART_DIRECTION 6.

Hard exclusions: no color gradients in sections, no drop shadows, no inverted colour bands, no three identical feature cards, no Lucide/FontAwesome/Material icons, no Inter/Roboto/Arial/Open Sans/Helvetica, no emoji, no stock photography, no fake dashboards with random numbers, no infinite-loop animations, no edge-to-edge sticky navbar glued to the top.

## 4. Visual system (pinned; mockups and build must match)

Palette (one background for the whole page):
- Background `#121212` full page; cards and floats `#171717`
- Foreground `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` body text; foreground-2 `#a3a3a3` sublines and metadata (7:1 contrast on background)
- Rule `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` hairline borders on cards; no structural rules or section dividers
- Kiln `#b5502f` fills and decoration only; Ember `#e0805a` accent text (6.5:1 on background)
- No drop shadows. Depth from soft radial glows `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` in backgrounds and the hero gradient.
- Hero gradient background: CSS dusk (top `#232a37` → `#4f4f55` at 55% → `#3a2e29` at 85% → background), with film-grain overlay (SVG feTurbulence data-URI, 8% opacity, overlay blend).

Typography (all via next/font/google, no runtime font requests):
- Display and headings: Outfit (weights 400 and 500), no variable. H1 `clamp(3.25rem, 8vw, 6rem)` weight 400, line-height 1.02, tracking -0.01em. H2 `clamp(2.25rem, 4.5vw, 3.75rem)` weight 400, centred. Sublines: Outfit 400 at 1.25rem in foreground-2, centred, max 60 characters.
- Body: Outfit 400, 1.0625rem, foreground-2.
- Labels and numbers: Geist Mono, weights 400/500, for all metadata, vitals, indices.
- Arabic only: IBM Plex Sans Arabic for the chat still.

Layout: 8px base; container max 1200px; gutters 20px mobile, 40px desktop; section separation by whitespace (160px mobile to 224px desktop), no hairline rules between sections. Headings centred. Cards: `#171717` background, 1px rule border, 12px radius. Buttons: pills (48px tall), primary = solid foreground background with background text, secondary = 1px rule outline with foreground text. Everything collapses to one column under 768px. Imagery: typography, the system schematic (client → edge → API → queue → model / database), thinking orb as the hero's full stop, the hyperspace canvas on Billion word interaction, ghost wordmark in the footer. No photographs.

Nav: Two-state. Over the hero: tiny foreground-2 text links top-left, wordmark "Billion" centred in Outfit 500, pill "Talk to an engineer" top-right, all on a transparent bar. After hero scrolls out: floating dark pill nav (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.72)` + backdrop blur, 1px rule border) fades in, centred. Mobile: wordmark + Menu button, the existing sheet.

## 7. Motion (the animate skill decides the values; this is intent only)

All transforms, no opacity on text (LCP elements remain unpainted). Hero title "Think Bigger / Think Billion": each word rises from `translateY(0.25em)` into place over 700 ms with easing-out, 70 ms stagger. The schematic reveals itself on view with a clip-path wipe per stage (no stroke-dash, no pathLength). The timeline hairline is a div scaled with scroll progress (transform only). Section cards and items reveal once on scroll with transform-only entrance (no re-trigger). Chevron arrows nudge on hover. CTA buttons follow the pointer spring (magnetic) when fine-pointer media and motion allowed, at most 6 px travel. The thinking orb idles with a breathing cycle. The Hyperspace canvas runs once on page load, as soon as the hero is in view: it ramps to light speed, holds, then settles back to the static field over about 4 s total. Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion) makes every animation static. No JavaScript still renders the full page, including a sparse static starfield and all text.

## 8. Accessibility and mobile

Semantic landmarks, one H1, skip link, visible focus rings, AA contrast on every text/background pair (check Kiln on Paper for small text; use Ink for small text), alt text on every meaningful SVG, keyboard-operable mobile menu with focus trap and Escape. Mobile-native skill rules: `dvh` units, safe-area insets, no tap highlight flash, hover gated by `(hover: hover)`, 16px minimum input font, no horizontal scroll at 320px.
