# Billion landing page — design and content brief

Revised 2026-09-17 for the software-house pivot; see docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-16-software-house-pivot-design.md.

Single source of truth for every worker on this feature. If a task spec and this file disagree, this file wins. Do not edit this file; if something here is impossible, say so in your report.

## 1. Who Billion is

Billion is a software house based in Cairo whose standing point is performance optimized to the maximum, and whose promise is that it has a solution for anything it faces. Motto: **Think bigger. Think Billion.** Billion solves problems across multiple domains: AI products and agents, platforms and products, performance engineering, data and automation, industrial and operations software, and the hard problems nobody else wants. Industrial software is where Billion started and remains a specialty, but not the whole story. Valor Labs (valor-labs.com) is its sister company in agriculture, an Arabic-first site for Egyptian farmers and distributors whose headline is "نشأنا محليًا لنخدم عالميًا" (we grew locally to serve globally) and whose product includes an "Ask Our Engineer" AI assistant with weather tracking and market prices. Billion and Valor share the same local pride and engineer-not-salesman tone.

## 2. Audience and tone

Founders, product and engineering leads, and operations directors who need something built or made fast. They read spec sheets, not startup blogs. They care about shipping speed, reliability under load, cost at scale, and whether a vendor will still support the system in year three. They have been burned by consultants and platforms that shipped late or didn't scale.

Write plainly, in English, with specifics. Banned words: elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, game-changer, revolutionary, cutting-edge, empower, delve, synergy, "AI-powered" as a headline. No invented statistics, client names, or client locations. Claims about Billion's own process are fine; claims of results are not.

## 3. Design read (for design-taste-frontend)

Reading this as: B2B software house landing for founders and engineering leads, with a calm engineering-report editorial language, leaning toward Tailwind + Newsreader/Geist + restrained scroll motion.

Archetype: "Editorial Luxury" texture (high-end-visual-design) crossed with the warm-monochrome, flat, typographic system of minimalist-ui. Think a Swiss engineering firm's annual report or a machine datasheet, not a SaaS site. Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 6, VISUAL_DENSITY 4, ART_DIRECTION 7.

Hard exclusions: no purple/blue AI glow, no mesh or default gradients, no glassmorphism, no centered-hero-over-dark-mesh, no three identical feature cards, no pill buttons on primary actions, no Lucide/FontAwesome/Material icons, no Inter/Roboto/Arial/Open Sans/Helvetica, no emoji, no stock photography, no fake dashboards with random numbers, no infinite-loop animations, no edge-to-edge sticky navbar glued to the top.

## 4. Visual system (pinned; mockups and build must match)

Palette (one palette for the whole page; dark sections reuse it inverted):
- Paper `#F5F2EC` canvas; Paper-2 `#EDE8DF` alternate surface; White `#FFFFFF` card surface
- Ink `#15130F` display text and dark section background; Ink-2 `#2B2822` body text; Graphite `#6B665C` secondary text
- Rule `rgba(21,19,15,0.10)` hairlines; on dark: `rgba(245,242,236,0.14)`
- Kiln `#B5502F` single accent (links, WhatsApp button, numbered indices, one underline); Kiln-hover `#9E4327`; Kiln-tint `#F3E4DD` for tags
- Optional film-grain overlay at 3% opacity on paper sections. Shadows: none, or diffuse at <= 4% opacity.

Typography (all via next/font/google, no runtime font requests):
- Display: Newsreader (variable, optical size on), weight 400 to 500, tracking -0.02em, line-height 1.05. H1 `clamp(2.75rem, 6vw, 5.5rem)`, H2 `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`.
- Body and UI: Geist, 17px/1.6 body, 15px UI, weights 400/500.
- Metadata: Geist Mono, 12 to 13px, uppercase, tracking 0.08em, for eyebrows, indices ("01" to "07"), labels.

Layout: 8px base; container max 1280px; gutters 20px mobile, 40px desktop; section padding 96px mobile to 160px desktop; hairline rules as the main structural device; numbered sections; asymmetric grids allowed (bento for solution cards), everything collapses to one column under 768px with generous gaps. Corner radius 2 to 6px. Buttons: primary = solid Ink with Paper text; accent = solid Kiln with Paper text (WhatsApp only); secondary = hairline outline. Icons: inline SVG line primitives at 1.25px stroke, or Phosphor Light. Imagery: typography, color fields, hairline technical line drawings (system schematic showing client → edge → API → queue → model / database, thinking orb as the hero's full stop, outlined "BILLION" watermark in the hero, live-vitals performance band), and UI stills drawn in code. No photographs.

Nav: inset floating bar (not glued edge-to-edge), wordmark "Billion" set in Newsreader, links: What we build, Performance, How we work, Proof, and a Kiln WhatsApp button. Mobile: a full-height sheet menu.

## 7. Motion (the animate skill decides the values; this is intent only)

Hero text and panel: one staggered reveal on load. Sections: reveal once on scroll, translate no more than 12px, no re-trigger. Hover: near-imperceptible. Hover-only loops are allowed (e.g. card hover effects). The thinking orb has idle breathing animation. The hero paragraph and H1 are never opacity-animated (they are LCP elements). `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all of it. Cheapest tool that works; a motion library only if CSS cannot do it.

## 8. Accessibility and mobile

Semantic landmarks, one H1, skip link, visible focus rings, AA contrast on every text/background pair (check Kiln on Paper for small text; use Ink for small text), alt text on every meaningful SVG, keyboard-operable mobile menu with focus trap and Escape. Mobile-native skill rules: `dvh` units, safe-area insets, no tap highlight flash, hover gated by `(hover: hover)`, 16px minimum input font, no horizontal scroll at 320px.
